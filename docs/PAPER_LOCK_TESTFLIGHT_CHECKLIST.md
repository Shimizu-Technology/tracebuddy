# Paper Lock (Beta): TestFlight check

Leon: run this on your oldest available supported non-LiDAR iPhone and a supported iPad. Use the new TraceBuddy 1.3 TestFlight build. Keep a stand, ruler, stopwatch, Letter/A4 sheets and a printed calibration page with corner crosses and a 10 mm grid nearby.

The [Paper Lock release gate](REAL_DEVICE_TESTING.md#paper-lock-release-gate) is the source of truth for the test matrix and pass limits. This checklist helps you run it; it does not waive any gate. Leave Paper Lock in Beta until the measurements pass.

- [ ] Record device, iOS/iPadOS, TestFlight version/build, date, starting battery, table color and lighting. Use Letter and A4, portrait and landscape, on light and dark tables in ordinary indoor light. Add a current iPhone/LiDAR device if available.
- [ ] Check the picker: Camera + paper is still the default. Paper Lock says “Beta.” Pick a guide, complete parent setup and choose Surface Lock. Time ten fresh attempts per device; at least 9/10 must show a placeable guide within 5 seconds. Reset must bring back a visible preview before Lock. Point away from the table: the old preview must disappear and Lock must stop working.
- [ ] Print the in-app marker at 100%. Measure it: exactly 50 mm square. Keep its top upright, line its top edge up with the paper's top, and leave a 15 mm gap to the left of the paper. Attach it so it moves with the page. Choose Marker Lock. Time ten fresh attempts per device; at least 9/10 must recognize the whole marker within 3 seconds. The guide must lie flat on the page, with its top and bottom facing the right way.
- [ ] For both modes and every paper size/orientation, line up the calibration guide, Lock, and measure the ruler distance from each of the four guide crosses to its printed cross. Record all four errors in mm; the largest must be at most 8 mm for Surface or 5 mm for Marker. Use a local image of the matching calibration page as the guide if needed. Account for its inset: adjust Page scale/alignment before Lock, then leave the controls alone during measurements.
- [ ] On the stand, watch one cross for 10 seconds. Record its full back-and-forth movement; peak-to-peak jitter must be at most 3 mm. Move the device 10–20 cm and tilt about 20°. After it settles, measure the same four crosses again: at most 8 mm Surface / 5 mm Marker.
- [ ] Cover the page/marker with a hand or pencil for 2–3 seconds. The UI must leave the safe locked state and prevent child mode. Uncover it: recover or offer Reset within 8 seconds. Try Reset and Camera fallback. Slide the paper 5–10 cm and rotate it 15°: Marker should follow with its attached marker; Surface is table-anchored and needs relocking.
- [ ] Background and return, open/close Control Center, rotate the device, and try iPad multitasking. Each must recover within 8 seconds or offer a working Reset/Camera route. Try dim light, glare, a white table, fast motion, part of the marker hidden and the marker outside the frame; record whether guidance helps and stale guides disappear.
- [ ] Trace for 10 minutes in each mode. Record ending battery, heat, any crash, freeze longer than 1 second, or serious/critical thermal state if available from device diagnostics. If thermal state cannot be measured, mark it unverified. Do not count a short session as this test.

Record results in `output/tracebuddy-fix-2026-10-27/w3/device-results/` beside the W3 QA ledger, or send the measurements to Claude for that folder. Use one row per attempt/condition: device + OS + build, mode, paper + orientation, table/light, acquisition seconds, four errors before/after moving, jitter mm, recovery seconds, session/battery/heat, pass/fail and notes. Include photos/video where helpful; screenshots alone do not measure alignment. Mark anything you cannot run **not run**, with the reason. Any missed limit or unmeasured release condition stays open for the lead before App Store release.
