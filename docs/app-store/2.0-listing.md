## Description

TraceBuddy GU gives families a place to draw together. Pick a picture and choose Simple for bold, friendly lines or Detailed for more to trace. You’ll find animals, ocean life, vehicles, and pictures from Guam.

Draw right on your iPhone or iPad. Try a pencil, crayon, marker, or watercolor. Fill a space with one tap or use the Huge brush to color a big area. Pick a shade, undo a mark, and zoom in for small details. When you’re done, add a stamp and find your work again in My drawings.

In Learn to draw, watch a picture take shape one step at a time, then trace or copy each step. You can listen to the directions as you go. Practice letters and numbers, type a name or phrase in My words, or choose a photo of your own to draw from.

To trace on real paper, use On paper. Prop up your iPhone or iPad above the page, line up the guide over the live camera view, and draw with a real pencil. When you’re finished, tap Done and choose Snap your drawing to keep a photo of it in My drawings.

Come back to a drawing later or choose Save to Photos to keep a copy. Grown-ups can use the protected settings to back up drawings, lesson progress, and favorites to a file and restore them later.

Drawing, tracing, and lessons work offline. Your work is stored on your device, and you can choose where to save or share copies. There’s no account, no ads, and no tracking.

## App Review notes

No account or login is required.

1. On the first launch, choose Little artist or Big artist. Pick a picture from Home and tap On the screen to try the drawing tools. Tap Done to finish and open My drawings to find the saved work.
2. To try On paper, use an iPhone or iPad with a working camera and a sheet of paper on a flat surface. Open a picture, choose On paper, and allow camera access. Position the device above the page and line up the guide with the paper.
3. Finish On paper by tapping Done. If the guide is locked, press and hold the lock icon to unlock it first. Tap Snap your drawing, tap the shutter, then tap Keep it to save the photo in My drawings.
4. From Home, open Grown-ups and press and hold Open grown-up settings for two seconds. With VoiceOver, activate the button and select 9 for “What is 4 + 5?” Back up drawings opens the system share sheet. Restore drawings opens the system file picker so you can choose a TraceBuddy backup.

## App Privacy

- **Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** No. Expected label **Data Not Collected**.
- **Tracking and advertising identifier** No.
- **Privacy policy** https://tracebuddy-gu.netlify.app/privacy.html

The iOS Release app uses bundled art and fonts, on-device speech, private local storage, and the system camera, photo picker, and file picker. The mobile source and dependency audit found no production analytics, ads, crash-reporting SDK, account backend, remote processing, or automatic app upload. The picture loader’s `fetch` reads bundled art on web only. On iOS it reads a local file. Save to Photos and the system share sheet send a copy to a destination the user chooses. Photos or Files can sync according to the user’s device settings. [Apple App Privacy Details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)

## Build handoff

Use `production` for the submission build. The marketing version is `2.0`. EAS sets and increments the iOS build number remotely, so the local Simulator bundle’s build number is separate. Read the current number with `eas build:version:get --platform ios --profile production` before building. Confirm the build processed for 2.0 is newer than TestFlight build 44. [Expo version management](https://docs.expo.dev/build-reference/app-versions/)

The production switch for Stick to paper is `build.production.env.EXPO_PUBLIC_PAPER_LOCK` in `mobile/eas.json`. It defaults to `0`. Use `testflight-2` to test the enabled feature and complete [the physical acceptance checklist](../TRACEBUDDY_2_0.md#paper-lock-before-enabling) before turning the production switch on.
