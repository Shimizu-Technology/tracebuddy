TraceBuddy

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

TraceBuddy lets you trace and draw on an iPhone, iPad, or in a web browser. TraceBuddy has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Shimizu Technology does not receive your camera feed, photos, words, drawings, or backups through the app.

Camera

On paper uses a live camera preview to show a tracing guide over your paper. TraceBuddy does not record video or save the frames from the live preview. In the iPhone and iPad app, Snap your drawing takes a still photo when you tap the shutter. Choose Keep it to save the photo in My drawings. Camera processing takes place on your device.

Photos

My photo opens your photo library so you can choose a picture to use. TraceBuddy can keep a private copy with your drawing so you can open it again later. Choose Save to Photos to put a copy of a drawing or a paper photo in your photo library. You can change camera and photo access in your device’s settings.

Drawings and settings

TraceBuddy saves work such as your drawings, custom words, selected photos, favorites, lesson progress, preferences, and tracing-guide setup in private app storage on iPhone and iPad. The web app uses browser storage and caches app files for offline use. Tracing-guide setup stores details such as position and size.

You can delete a drawing in My drawings. Earlier app versions and the web app offer Clear local work. Deleting the app or clearing the site’s browser data removes its local private storage. To remove copies you exported, delete them from Photos, Files, or wherever you saved them.

Backups, exports, and printing

In Grown-ups, Back up drawings prepares a file that can include your drawings, photos kept with them, lesson progress, and favorites. You choose where it goes using the system share sheet, including a folder in Files. Restore drawings uses the system file picker to read the backup you select. TraceBuddy does not need general access to your files.

Saving an image, printing a worksheet, or sharing a file happens when you choose that action. TraceBuddy does not upload your work. Files and Photos may sync according to your device settings, and a share destination you choose may send a copy outside your device.

Data collection and children’s privacy

TraceBuddy is designed for families and individual use. It does not collect personal data from children or adults, and it does not use advertising identifiers or third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
